# l06 notes

## (a) Forms I was less than fully sure of
- 1pl/2pl subjunctive of "come": written بیایم / بیاین, transliterated biyāyim / biyāyin (check against the transliteration lesson 2 used; spoken it is close to biyāym / biyāyn). Likewise mikhāyim / mikhāyin.
- "o" for "and": fin "o", fa "و" (spoken o, or -o after a vowel, e.g. beram o). Not taught as a clitic.
- yā (یا) written fin "yā" (not "ya") to match speech.
- nemikhām / nemitūnam used alone after vali (verb elided) - natural, but flagged in notes.
- lāzeme skipped (did not fit cleanly); "ke" not taught.

## (b) Teaching order
bāyad -> bāyad + beram/biyām/bokhoram -> other persons (bāyad invariant) -> nabāyad -> vali -> chon (with chera? / chon bāyad beram) -> yā (with a question) -> o -> long chained prompts -> review of 8 chained sentences.

## (c) New vocabulary
bāyad باید, nabāyad نباید, vali ولی, chon چون, yā یا, o / va و. (Reused: chera, emruz, fardā, emshab, alān, injā, unjā.)
