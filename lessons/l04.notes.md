# l04 notes

## (a) Forms I was less than fully sure of
- raftī / raftim / raftin / raftan: transliterated 2sg ending as "ī"; spoken it is often short "i". Plural -in/-an colloquial (formal -īd/-and).
- nemikhāstam beram (subjunctive after past 'want') is correct colloquially; some prefer nemikhāstam beram over nemikhāstam berm. Kept full.
- Negative of omadan (nayomadam / niyomadam) deliberately NOT taught, spelling/pronunciation varies.
- "vali" (ولی) taught as 'but'; "bokhoram vali nemitūnestam" order is natural.
- Past 3sg bare stem raft is fine; pronoun dropped (could be he or she).
- Phrases with a comma ("Yesterday I went, tomorrow I'm going") have no comma in fa/fin (join by space rule).

## (b) Teaching order
mikhāstam -> mikhāstam beram (contrast mikhām beram) -> nemikhāstam beram -> raftam -> dirūz raftam -> contrast fardā miram -> naraftam -> omadam -> dishab -> khordam / nakhordam -> 3sg raft -> 1pl omadim -> 2sg raftī -> 3pl raftan -> nemitūnestam bokhoram -> vali -> review.

## (c) New vocabulary
mikhāstam, nemikhāstam, raftam, naraftam, omadam, khordam, nakhordam, mitūnestam/nemitūnestam, dishab, dirūz, vali, plus past 3sg raft, omadim, raftī, raftan.
