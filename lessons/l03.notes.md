# l03 notes

## (a) Forms not fully sure of
- miyām / nemiyām / miyād / nemiyād / miyāyim spelled without ZWNJ (میام, نمیام, میاد, نمیاد, میایم): normal colloquial spelling, so the "ZWNJ after می" rule is not applied to them.
- miyāyim (we come): spoken often as miyāym / miyān-e-mā; parts split yā + yim is a teaching simplification. Check lesson 2's "we" form of come for consistency.
- mire / mikhore / miyād (he/she) in Persian script: می‌ره, می‌خوره, میاد.
- "dāram" with a future time word is not used; the lesson says dāram is only for right now.

## (b) Teaching order
be- to mi- swap for miram, miyām, mikhoram; contrast with mikhām + be- form; place before verb; negatives; alān; fardā (present = near future); emruz; emshab; he/she and we once each; dāram + verb; vali; long sentence; mixed review of 8. 41 steps plus review (over the 28-36 target, can trim prompts).

## (c) New vocabulary
miram, miyām, mikhoram, nemiram, nemiyām, nemikhoram, alān, emruz, fardā, emshab, dāram, vali, plus miyād, miyāyim.
