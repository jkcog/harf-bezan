# l02 notes

## Unsure forms
- 2p/1p "-ym/-yn" after a vowel (mikhāym, biyāym, mikhāyn, biyāyn): written می‌خوایم / می‌خواین, بیایم / بیاین; spoken often mikhāyn ~ mikhāin.
- 1p of "come" (biyāym) and 2p (biyāyn) appear only in hints/glosses, not drilled as separate teach steps.
- "un" used as the stress pronoun for he/she (not "u").
- Ids: review items take ids before the review step's own id (same as l01).

## Teaching order
you (mikhāy, beri, mitūni, bokhori) -> he/she (mikhād, bere, biyād, mitūne, bokhore) -> stress pronouns man/to/un -> we (mikhāym, berim, mitūnim, bokhorim) -> you all (mikhāyn, berin, shomā, biyāyn) -> they (mikhān, beran, mitūnan, bokhoran, unā) -> mixed review (7 items).

## New vocabulary
Endings -y/-i (you), -d/-e (he/she), -ym/-im (we), -yn/-in (you all), -n/-an (they); pronouns man, to, un, mā, shomā, unā; question by intonation.
